# Coverage view and routing view

Scope: the two screens of DesirePath. `/coverage` is Persuade, `/` is Operate.
Audience: organisers and researchers in a bright room, residents on a phone outdoors.
Task: understand in five seconds that the red pavement is counted as covered and cannot
be reached; then, on the routing view, confirm a profile before anything routes.
Constraints: bright projector, nothing carried by colour alone, no horizontal scroll at
390px, full keyboard operation on the card.

## Direction contract

THESIS: DesirePath is the municipal street-work notice the city tapes to your lamppost,
turned back on the city: the same stencil weight and barricade language used to report
what the quarter-mile promise actually delivers. It refuses the dark basemap with a
floating translucent panel that every heat-equity dashboard ships, and it refuses the
warm editorial data-story with a serif headline.

OWN-WORLD: One saturated slate field (#10263F) edge to edge, the map knocked out of it.
Bone (#F5F1E8) for type, high-visibility yellow (#FFC400) for the shortfall and nothing
else, cold cyan (#3BB8D4) for what is reachable. Hatch and dash are the second channel
everywhere, so no fill reads by hue alone. Overpass, heavy, at sign scale. Square corners,
no translucency, no rounded cards. Every ink measured against every ground it sits on.

STORY: The viewer reads the city's own promise, sees the map disagree with it, and leaves
able to say the number and the neighbourhood.

FIRST VIEWPORT: No container, no page margin. One line at the top: the neighbourhood, and
the gap figure in yellow as the largest ink on the screen. The map fills everything below,
full bleed, shortfall hatched in yellow over reachable in cyan. Bottom: one recessed strip
carrying the three readings with what each is down from, the element count, and the DEP
claim as attribution at the size attribution is. Neighbourhood switching is a row of square
tabs on the plate.

FORM: NYC DOT street-work notice and stencil vernacular. Candidate 3 of 7 on the
resonance-ordered grounded list. Seed key 6df52257, re-roll round 1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review,
the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
